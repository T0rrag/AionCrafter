01 / Product scope

Two workflows. One price model.

Build the economic decision, not a game automation tool. The same calculations should work in a browser, desktop window or approved overlay.

01
Workflow A / Item economics

“What does this item cost to make?”

1Select a crafted product. Match region, recipe version, output variant and quantity.
2Price its materials. Show ingredients, unit prices, subtotals and optional sub-crafts.
3Compare the output. Show a suitable selling-price reference, sale fees and estimated profit/loss.

For an item already owned, this estimates replacement economics. Actual historical profit needs the player’s recorded costs and sale proceeds.

Workflow B / Material price checker

“The game already gives me the recipe.”

1Enter or paste a materials list. Confirm item matches and quantities.
2Select the market. Reuse the active region/server settings.
3Return the shopping total. Show only material prices and acquisition cost. No recipe or output price is required.

“Current” must mean a dated observation from the selected market—not an old saved number with a fresh page-load timestamp.

Out of scope: an in-game Lua addon, automatic purchasing/listing/crafting, custom game-client injection, packet capture and memory reading. An overlay is a presentation option, not a price source. [08] [10]

Verification limits and correction to the earlier feasibility discussion

The existence of NC’s developer service and third-party recipe tools does not prove that NC publishes an AION 2 auction API. The earlier example GET /market/items/{item_id}?server=EU-01 was illustrative, not an endpoint discovered or tested.

This audit used public documentation and provider pages. No authenticated auction response, item-level price or in-game comparison was obtained. Direct HTTP requests from the execution environment failed DNS resolution; JavaScript-only directories could not be fully inspected through the available web reader. No client network traffic was inspected. These limits do not establish that an API is absent.

Current disposition: manual/reference-price MVP = proceed; community snapshots = investigate with permission; automatic fresh AH feed = unresolved; Overwolf compatibility = unresolved.

Recommended next evidence

Ask for an integration contract.

Request a sample for the pilot market, source observation times, stable item IDs, listing-versus-sales semantics, quantity/depth fields, update cadence, API quotas and explicit storage/display/redistribution permission.

The provider’s public contact route is available. No message has been sent. [15]

Price-source hierarchy / proposed

Prefer permission and traceability.

First: a documented NC feed, if confirmed.
Second: a licensed community snapshot/API.
Fallback: the user’s observations, permitted imports or applicable vendor prices—with source and age labels.

Do not advertise “live” by hiding delays or by mixing market, vendor and manually entered values.

03 / Independent dependencies

Two gates. Neither blocks the core.

Gate A controls automatic prices. Gate B controls the Overwolf edition. The manual calculator and a regular browser/desktop view are separate deliverables.

“Surgeforge” is treated here as a likely reference to CurseForge, the addon/mod distribution ecosystem. Overwolf is the app/overlay development route being evaluated. No AION 2 addon runtime is assumed. [08] [14]

06 / Calculation specification

Profit is only as sound as its inputs.

Show the assumptions beside the answer. Unknown costs, unverified probabilities and stale market references must remain visible.

06

Deterministic batch economics

crafts = ceil(target_units / yield)
cost = Σ acquisition_cost(inputs) + craft_fees
net = sell_units × sell_price × (1 − tax)
     − listing_and_other_sale_fees
profit = net − cost
ROI = profit / cost × 100

For the simple case with one output, a fixed total cost and positive net-of-tax rate:

break_even_unit_price =
(cost + sale_fees) / [sell_units × (1 − tax)]

Use exact arithmetic and the market’s verified rounding. Treat unsold leftovers explicitly; do not assume every output sells. ROI is undefined when its cost denominator is zero.

Stochastic recipes and honest prices

expected_profit_per_attempt =
Σ [outcome_probability × net_outcome_value]
− expected_cost_per_attempt

For mutually exclusive outcomes, probabilities must sum to one. Bonus outputs need a separate model. Do not insert a universal “25% proc” assumption.

Missing priceUnknown total or clearly marked partial subtotal; not zero.
Owned materialsSeparate additional cash needed from their economic value.
Listed priceAn asking-price reference, not proof of completed sales.

08 / Decision log

Keep the assumptions explicit.

Scope decisions are firm; implementation choices and external integrations remain proposals until supported by evidence.

08
D-01

No WoW-style in-game addon

Use an external companion; an overlay is acceptable. Do not make an AION 2 addon environment a requirement.

User decision
D-02

Core engine independent of market provider

Manual and approved automated sources share one observation contract. A provider failure should not invalidate the whole application.

Proposed
D-03

Do not promise live prices yet

Keep Gate A unresolved until there is authorized access, a usable sample and measured freshness. A marketing claim is not an integration test.

Open evidence
D-04

Overwolf is conditional, not mandatory

The current Native onboarding page requires proposal/whitelisting and describes public-app and monetization conditions; it says private apps are not approved. Review this fit before adopting the platform. [12]

Open evidence
D-05

Single-market pilot before broader coverage

Region, server/market group, faction and build must be selected explicitly. Do not mix Taiwan, Korea or Global data because item names look alike.

To decide
D-06

Safety and data rights are release conditions

Prefer a documented publisher feed or a contracted provider. Keep custom packet capture, memory reading, client injection and game automation outside this project’s scope.
